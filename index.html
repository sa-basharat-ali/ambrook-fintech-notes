01Bill Pay · Invoicing · payments network

Fraud on the payment rails as trucking grows

Ambrook today

More than 1,000 trucking customers and a payments network in progress. Trucking payments are a known fraud target: double brokering, carrier identity theft, redirected payments. Farm cash flow is seasonal, so fixed rules raise a lot of false alarms.

At Geidea

Before I joined, the only fraud caught was what the processor flagged. I clustered merchant transaction behavior to find the cases it missed, for example a grocery store that runs one very large sale, cancels it or switches the terminal off partway through, then asks for a refund. I also built real-time anomaly checks on 5 to 15M transactions a day.

I'd build first

Behavior baselines for each vertical, because a feed store and a freight carrier shouldn't share thresholds. Monitoring of ACH returns and new payees on Bill Pay and Invoicing. KYB signals at onboarding for the new verticals.

02Wallet · Card

The cost of freezing a good customer

Ambrook today

A held Wallet or a declined card in the middle of harvest or calving costs far more trust than it would at a typical software company. Ambrook's reputation is built on hands-on support.

What I found

Most freeze policies are tuned on model accuracy alone. Once I priced in the good customers who get frozen and how long each review takes, the best policy changed.

I'd build first

A hold and review policy measured in dollars, with review time treated as part of the product.

03Bill Pay · Card

Moving checks to card and ACH

Ambrook today

Bill Pay still mails paper checks. Every bill that moves to ACH or the Ambrook Visa earns payment revenue and also gives the books cleaner data.

At Geidea

I analyzed declines and approval rates by merchant category code (MCC), and found that wrong MCC assignments explained approval gaps. I segmented 40K+ merchants by MCC, interchange and chargebacks, and built the dashboards behind interchange optimization and FinOps reconciliation.

I'd build first

A funnel from check to ACH to card, by vendor and by vertical. Approval and decline analysis for ag co-op, fuel and feed MCCs. A mapping from MCC to Schedule F lines so card spend gets categorized automatically.

Mailed check→ACH→Ambrook Visa

Each step to the right means more revenue per bill and cleaner data for the books. The funnel would show where each vendor and vertical gets stuck.

04Operators · customer success

Keeping customers while growing 3x

Ambrook today

Customers roughly tripled in a year, and white-glove support gets harder at every step up in size.

At Geidea

I built merchant churn prediction behind a tiered retention strategy and designed A/B tests of retention offers. Churn went from 35% to about 10%. I also ran LTV, CAC and cohort analysis across 400K+ POS terminals.

I'd build first

An account health score that tells customer success who to call this week. Cohort retention split by vertical and by whether the customer uses Wallet.

05Wallet · the books

Keeping the books and the money in sync

Ambrook today

The promise is that money and books live in one place. That holds only if Treasury and Issuing events, bank feeds and the ledger agree.

At Geidea

I re-engineered the settlement and onboarding data models, and my anomaly checks caught settlement errors before they reached reporting.

I'd build first

Daily per-account checks that tie the payment feeds to the ledger, reported as gross error, because netted totals hide problems.

06The books · AI CFO

Measuring AI bookkeeping by vertical

Ambrook today

AI transaction tagging and document extraction, an MCP server, and an AI CFO on the roadmap, all now spreading into new verticals.

What I found

One overall accuracy number can look fine while every segment underneath it is wrong. A trucking book and a cattle book probably fail in different ways.

I'd build first

Tagging accuracy reported for each vertical and each category, using a small human-labeled sample to check how the evals score.
